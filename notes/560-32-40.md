# [[560,32,40]] weight-16 cyclic generalized-bicycle

## Direction & hypothesis

The gain here is row weight alone, and the note says so plainly because the parameters otherwise look worse than an entry filed the same day. The board's two entries at n <= 560 with k >= 32 and d >= 40 are `codes/420-32-40.json` at row weight 18 and `codes/502-102-50.json` at row weight 24. This code matches the first on k and d at a larger blocklength, and beats it on weight at 16, which is the axis that leaves it undominated. The ring is m = 280, divisible by 5 and 7, admitting the residue construction that fixes k without constructing the gcd.

## What was searched

Sparse polynomial pairs on x^280 - 1, with k = 2 deg gcd(a, b, x^m - 1) computed by Euclid so most draws die before any matrix work, and survivors priced against the quotient-norm lift, the single-block ideal of (x^m - 1)/gcd(a, x^m - 1), and the symmetric fold of `research/foldbound.py`. Of 26 Pareto-distinct points from this harvest the fold refuted 17, the refuted set being dominated by claims sitting exactly on the quotient-norm lift cap 2m/q.

## Evidence trail

The claim is a witness-backed upper bound, with weight-40 witnesses on both sides.

    research/foldbound.py, uncapped          40   (tight: matches the witness)
    random information set, 24 seeds x 2e6   78   (median 84, maximum 86)
    verify/validate_candidate.py             passed, not refuted

The fold is tight, exhibiting a weight-40 logical of its own at two blocks with t = 14 and orbit size 20. The gate reports no distance-only peers and no shared Weisfeiler-Leman signature, so no peer re-measurement is owed.

## Dead ends

The high scorers in this pool were all claims at the lift cap and all collapsed: [[560,32,80]] at kd^2/n = 365.7 and [[560,26,80]] at 297.1 both fold to 40. A claim at the cap means the lift found its own best witness and stopped, and a fold at a divisor of m beats it. Four earlier entries of mine at that cap were corrected by maintainers on 2026-10-08 for this reason.

The fold does not subsume the trial-based search. A sibling candidate at m = 175 cleared the fold at exactly 40, fell to 38 under the confirmation search and then to 36 under the 24-seed scan, and was dropped rather than filed: three instruments each found something lighter and nothing indicated 36 was the floor.
