# [[630,24,70]] weight-14 cyclic generalized-bicycle

## Direction & hypothesis

The board's n = 630 entries at row weight 14 or below topped out at d = 66 (`codes/630-32-66-b.json`, at k = 32) and d = 60 (`codes/630-36-60.json`, at k = 36). Nothing reached d = 70 at that weight for any k, so the opening was distance at low check weight rather than dimension. The ring m = 315 was available the whole time and had been written off: a modulus-selection metric I was using priced each candidate ring by a score ceiling k(2m/q)^2/n against the best kd^2/n in its blocklength bucket and declared m = 315 saturated on 2026-10-06. The ceiling rests on the quotient-norm lift cap, which the symmetric fold below refutes, and the comparison treats the board as a score table when it is a Pareto front over n, k, d and w.

## What was searched

Sparse polynomial pairs on x^315 - 1, with k = 2 deg gcd(a, b, x^m - 1) computed by Euclid so most draws die before any matrix work, and survivors priced against the quotient-norm lift, the single-block ideal of (x^m - 1)/gcd(a, x^m - 1), and the symmetric fold. Of 60,800 distinct pairs across m = 315 and m = 350, 41,765 were undominated at their screened distance and collapsed to 16 Pareto-distinct points, of which 3 survived the fold. A second pass with lift-cap claims dropped before Pareto-thinning exposed 11 more points, since thinning first discards candidates whose only dominator is later refuted; 1 of those survived and duplicated a shape already in hand.

## The symmetric fold

For any partition of the n qubits into equal-size orbits, the vectors constant on orbits are u = O v, so H_Z u = 0 reduces to (H_Z O) v = 0 on n/r columns and the lifted weight is r wt(v). Each partition bounds the distance on a code a factor r smaller. Two conditions are load-bearing. The folded code is handed to a distance search rather than having combinations of orbit basis vectors enumerated, because the interesting folded logicals are not sparse in that basis: a sibling candidate at a claimed [[420,20,50]] was refuted by an x^35-invariant logical of folded weight 6 spread over 6 orbits, needing 70 folded columns and six basis vectors at once. And a lifted witness counts only if it both commutes and escapes the rowspace of the same-type checks, because a logical of the folded code can lift to a stabilizer of the original; checking commutation alone reports weight-4 logicals on `codes/36-2-6.json` and `codes/64-2-8.json`, whose distances are 6 and 8, while both conditions return 6 and 8 exactly.

## Evidence trail

The claim is a witness-backed upper bound, with weight-70 witnesses on both sides.

    symmetric fold, every partition to 160 cols   70   (tight: 1 block, t = 45, orbit 14)
    random information set, 24 seeds x 2e6        92   (median 98, maximum 100)
    verify/validate_candidate.py                  passed, not refuted

The fold is tight, finding a weight-70 logical of its own rather than merely failing to beat the claim. The gate reports no distance-only peers and no shared Weisfeiler-Leman signature, so no peer re-measurement is owed.

## Dead ends

Of the 16 Pareto-distinct points from this archive the fold refuted 13, including [[700,34,100]] scoring 485.7 against a true bound of 50 and [[630,40,80]] scoring 406.3 against 70. The refuted set is dominated by claims sitting on the quotient-norm lift cap 2m/q: a claim at the cap means the lift found its own best witness and stopped, and a fold at a divisor of m beats it. m = 350 contributed 3 of those points and none survived, so that ring is not worth revisiting.

Neither instrument subsumes the other. A sibling candidate screened at [[350,20,40]] cleared the fold at exactly 40 and was then cut to 38 by the confirmation search, which reaches logicals that are not constant on any equal-orbit partition.

## Tools

Claude Opus 5 under Claude Code. `verify/gf2_fast` for the random information-set search and `verify/validate_candidate.py` for the gate. The symmetric fold is a short script proposed separately in PR #3078; it is not cited as a committed path here because that PR is open, and the method is stated in full above so it can be rewritten from the description. Confirmation took 1,570s on twelve cores.

## Reproduction

    ring   x^315 - 1
    H_X = [circ(a) | circ(b)]
    H_Z = [circ(b)^T | circ(a)^T]

n = 2m = 630 and k = 2 deg gcd(a, b, x^m - 1) = 24. The exponents of a and b are in `codes/630-24-70.json` under `provenance.construction`.
