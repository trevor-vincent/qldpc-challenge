# [[616,6,34]] — weight-6 quadricycle (rank-4 generalized bicycle) on Z_7×Z_11×Z_2×Z_2

## Direction & hypothesis

Target: the weight-6 × unrestricted CSS cell, high-distance corner. The repo's
quadricycle fieldnote (2026-09-19) left an open question: do rank-4 periodic
generalized bicycles — four *independent* shifts on an abelian 4-torus, as
opposed to the dependent-variable trivariate codes of arXiv:2406.19151 that
reduce to rank-2 — reach board territory that plain bivariate-bicycle sweeps
no longer find? Its pilot found a [[630,2,21]] but the planned sweep was
interrupted. Hypothesis: completing the sweep at weight-3 supports per side
(check weight 6) would surface undominated high-d points.

## What was searched

4,000 random quadricycle candidates via `research/quadricycle.py`
(`sample_quadricycle`): 2,000 at weight-2 supports (check weight 4, seed 31)
and 2,000 at weight-3 supports (check weight 6, seed 32), dims in [2,11] with
n = 2·l1·l2·l3·l4 ≤ 700. Screen: 1,500 fast-RIS trials, keeping candidates
whose screen efficiency beat their cell bar (eff > 2.0 at w4, > 6.0 at w6).
Ladder: 200k then 2M trials (fresh seeds); 66 survivors; full trusted gate
(`verify/validate_candidate.py`, refute=True) on each.

## Evidence trail

B = [(1,10,1,1), (5,2,0,1), (0,5,0,1)] (terms as 4-exponent monomials over

Gate verdict at submission time: `board_advancing: true`, gains on d and n,
`d_only_gain: false`, no dominators, no exact-fingerprint or WL match.

## Dead ends

- Of 4,000 candidates: 66 ladder survivors, **only 3 board-advancing**
  ([[450,4,30]], [[504,4,32]], and this code — all weight-6, all in the
  high-d/low-k corner); 63 passed the gate but landed dominated.
- **Weight-4 quadricycles: nothing.** No weight-2-support candidate beat the
  surface-code line (eff 2.0) after deep measurement — rank-4 at w4 appears
  barren in this n range.
- Sibling caution: the three survivors interlock ([[504,4,32]] would be
  dominated by [[450,4,30]] if its d sagged by 2), so each sibling needs its
  own deep ladder before any submission.
