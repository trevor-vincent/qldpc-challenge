# [[360,152,4]] hyperbolic surface code, □(3,4,3,4) quotient of order 360

## Direction & hypothesis

weight-8 x unrestricted. Nothing at n<=360 in the cell combines k>=152 with
d>=4 (nearest: [[338,104,4]] at k=104).

## What was searched

27 finite quotients of the triangle groups △(r,s,t) and the quadrilateral
groups □(r,s,t,u) (arXiv:2610.03682's L=1 families), enumerated
exhaustively/sample-wise as transitive generating tuples of involutions in
S_deg for degrees 6-9 with exact corner-rotation orders (the smooth-surface
condition), built into CSS codes, verified against kit css.verify_css, and
screened at 400 RIS trials (numpy backend). 12 quotients fell in the
n=200-1000 band, 6 were Pareto-non-dominated in their cell against all 1940
CSS board entries and were staged; each survivor then ran a fresh-seed
confirmation ladder (500k + 3x1M trials per side, gf2_fast accelerator) before
promotion.

## Dead ends

- degree-by-degree enumeration at degrees <= 5: no exact-order quotient of any
  paper tiling exists there; PSL(2,7) (order 168, degree 7) is the only small
  (4,4,4) quotient and it falls below the n=200 band
- direct products of known quotients: the product tiling's systole is the
  minimum over the factors, so low-d building blocks (Bolza, d=2) poison the
  distance no matter the product's size
- the paper's tabulated |G|=648 (k=328) and |G|=672 (k=172) instances did not
  appear within the sweep budgets; full degree-8 exhaustion of △(6,6,6) and
  △(4,4,4) was still running at promotion time

## Reproduction

Permutations of {0..5} written as image tuples (images of 0,1,..., in order):
a=[0, 1, 3, 2, 5, 4], b=[0, 2, 1, 3, 5, 4], c=[1, 0, 3, 2, 4, 5], d=[1, 0, 2,
4, 3, 5]. Group them: G = <a, b, c, d> under function composition has order
360, and the corner-rotation orders are exactly (ab, bc, cd, da) = (3, 4, 3,
4), which is also the freeness (smooth-surface) condition. Tiles are the 360
elements of G; a qubit sits on each tile. Corner c of tile g is identified
with corner c of tile g∘s for the two sides s adjacent to that corner (corner
c lies on sides c and c+1 mod 4), so each vertex's stabilizer support is the
set of tiles of its corners. The vertex graph is bipartite; X checks sit on
one colour class and Z on the other. n=360; k = n - rank(H_X) - rank(H_Z) over
GF(2). The staged entry's exact matrices are the authority; this recipe
rebuilds them deterministically.
