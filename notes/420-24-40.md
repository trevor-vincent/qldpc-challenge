# [[420,24,40]] weight-14 cyclic generalized-bicycle

## Direction & hypothesis

The board reached d >= 40 at n <= 420 and row weight 14 or below only at low dimension: `codes/344-6-40.json` at k = 6, `codes/400-12-40.json` at k = 12 and `codes/402-6-50.json` at k = 6. Nothing combined that distance and weight with k above 12, so the opening was dimension at fixed distance and light checks. The ring is m = 210, divisible by 5 and 7, admitting the residue construction that fixes k without constructing the gcd.

## What was searched

Sparse polynomial pairs on x^210 - 1, with k = 2 deg gcd(a, b, x^m - 1) computed by Euclid so most draws die before any matrix work, and survivors priced against the quotient-norm lift, the single-block ideal of (x^m - 1)/gcd(a, x^m - 1), and the symmetric fold of `research/foldbound.py`. The m = 210 shard has produced 93,821 harvest rows.

## Evidence trail

The claim is a witness-backed upper bound, with weight-40 witnesses on both sides.

    research/foldbound.py, uncapped          40   (tight: 2 blocks, t = 21, orbit 10)
    random information set, 24 seeds x 2e6   52   (median 56, maximum 58)
    verify/validate_candidate.py             passed, not refuted

The fold is tight, exhibiting a weight-40 logical of its own rather than merely failing to beat the claim, and the trial-based search stays above at 52. The gate reports no distance-only peers and no shared Weisfeiler-Leman signature, so no peer re-measurement is owed.

## Dead ends

The high scorers in this pool were claims sitting on the quotient-norm lift cap 2m/q and collapsed under the fold: [[420,30,60]] at kd^2/n = 257.1 folds to 30 and [[420,32,56]] at 238.9 folds to 28. Of 26 Pareto-distinct points from this harvest the fold refuted 17. A claim at the cap means the lift found its own best witness and stopped, and a fold at a divisor of m beats it.

The fold does not subsume the trial-based search in the other direction. A sibling candidate at m = 175 cleared the fold at exactly 40, fell to 38 under the confirmation search and then to 36 under the 24-seed scan, and was dropped rather than filed, since three instruments each found something lighter and nothing indicated 36 was the floor.
