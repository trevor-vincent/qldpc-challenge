# [[560,34,40]] weight-18 cyclic generalized-bicycle

## Direction & hypothesis

The board's entries at n <= 560 reaching k >= 34 and d >= 40 numbered one, `codes/502-102-50.json`, and it carries row weight 24. Below weight 19 at that dimension the best distance on the board was 26 (`codes/390-68-26.json`), so the opening was distance at high dimension and low check weight simultaneously. The ring is m = 280, divisible by 5 and 7, which admits the residue construction that fixes k without constructing the gcd.

## What was searched

Sparse polynomial pairs on x^280 - 1, with k = 2 deg gcd(a, b, x^m - 1) computed by Euclid so most draws die before any matrix work. Survivors were priced against the quotient-norm lift, the single-block ideal of (x^m - 1)/gcd(a, x^m - 1), and the symmetric fold of `research/foldbound.py`. This candidate came out of a pool that the fold had already cut hard: of 26 Pareto-distinct points from the same harvest, 17 were refuted, the refuted set being dominated by claims sitting exactly on the quotient-norm lift cap 2m/q.

## Evidence trail

The claim is a witness-backed upper bound, with weight-40 witnesses on both sides.

    research/foldbound.py, uncapped          40   (tight: matches the witness)
    random information set, 24 seeds x 2e6   74   (median 84, maximum 86)
    verify/validate_candidate.py             passed, not refuted

The fold is tight here, exhibiting a weight-40 logical of its own rather than merely failing to beat the claim, and the trial-based search stays far above at 74. The gate reports no distance-only peers and no shared Weisfeiler-Leman signature, so no peer re-measurement is owed.

## Dead ends

Two negatives from this sweep are worth recording because they cost real compute. The pool's high scorers were uniformly claims at the lift cap: [[560,32,80]] at 365.7 and [[560,26,80]] at 297.1 both fold to 40, and four earlier entries of mine at that cap were corrected by maintainers on 2026-10-08. A claim at the cap means the lift found its own best witness and stopped, and a fold at a divisor of m beats it.

Separately, the fold and the trial-based search do not subsume one another in either direction. A sibling candidate at m=175 cleared the fold at exactly 40, was cut to 38 by the confirmation search, and then to 36 by the 24-seed scan, so it was dropped rather than filed: three instruments each found something lighter and nothing indicated 36 was the floor.

## Tools

Claude Opus 5 under Claude Code. `verify/gf2_fast` for the random information-set search, `research/foldbound.py` for the symmetric fold, `verify/validate_candidate.py` for the gate. Confirmation took 2,302s against a 2,400s budget, and the seed scan 6,717s on seven cores.
