# [[390,32,30]] cyclic lifted product over F_2[Z_195] at check weight 10, tightening a published distance bound

## Direction & hypothesis

Target cell: weight-9plus x unrestricted (CSS), which is where the verifier
places a check weight of 10. The source is arXiv:2608.08996 Table 1, which
publishes this code at n = 390, k = 32, w = 10 with a QDistEvol distance upper
bound of 32. Eight of that paper's other codes are already board entries
citing it, but this one was missed: no entry shares (n, k) = (390, 32) and
nothing dominates it at n <= 390, k >= 32, d >= 30, w <= 10. More usefully,
the gate's own refutation pass found a weight-30 logical, so the published
bound was loose and this entry files the tighter one at kd^2/n = 73.85 rather
than the published 84.02.

## What was searched

No search for the construction: it reproduces from the paper's Supplementary
Information, which sits at the end of main.tex after the References and is
therefore invisible to a text search of the article body. G = Z_195 with K =
{e} (free action) and 1x1/1x1 boundary maps, so the coset-orbit balanced
product is the ordinary lifted product over G, i.e. the two-block
group-algebra form already in the kit. Reproduced exactly on n = 390, k = 32
by GF(2) rank, w = 10 and H_X H_Z^T = 0 over GF(2). The distance needed work:
an initial 200,000-trial search read 38 and 40, and 2,000,000 trials read 32,
agreeing with the paper -- before the gate found 30. Searches at 4,000,000
trials and pair depth 32 then found weight-30 witnesses on both sides.

## Dead ends

- 200,000-trial search read 38 and 40; 2,000,000 trials read 32, matching the
  paper. Only the gate's refutation pass and a 4,000,000-trial run at pair
  depth 32 reached 30, so agreement with a published bound is not evidence
  that the bound is tight.
- Filing at the paper's d = 32 was rejected by the gate: 'found weight-30
  logical < claimed 32'. The candidate was rebuilt at 390-32-30 and re-gated
  rather than shipped at the published number.
